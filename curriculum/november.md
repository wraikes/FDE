# November — Should I build it?

~30 hours. All practice, no reading. Coding is secondary.

Pick 2 verticals: one from October's briefs, one where you can reach real humans.

**Weekly:** one simulated discovery call with Claude in character, producing
1. workflow map
2. scoping doc
3. **priced proposal**

~6 calls total. Critiqued against `rubrics/`.

Pricing and proposal writing get equal weight with discovery. Knowing a workflow saves
$40k/yr is useless if you can't turn it into a number someone signs.

## Exit criteria
"I understand these businesses well enough to have an intelligent conversation with an
owner" — and to put a price on the work.
