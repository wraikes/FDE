# November — Should I build it?

~30 hours. Client discovery is the subject. All practice, no reading.
Lessons in `lessons/` (10-13), same format as October: method -> worked example -> you try.

Pick 2 verticals: one from October's briefs, one where you can reach real humans.

## The four modules

**10 — Discovery interviewing.** SPIN structure. Asking about the workflow before
proposing anything. Catching contradictions. Getting past the process they *believe*
they have to the one they run.

**11 — Workflow mapping.** Turning an hour of conversation into a map: steps, actors,
systems, handoffs, where the time and money leak.

**12 — Quantifying and ROI.** Putting a dollar figure on the current process. If you
can't, you can't price the new one.

**13 — Scoping and proposals.** Writing the thing someone signs. Value pricing, not
hourly. Scoping is saying no.

## Cadence

One simulated discovery call per week with Claude in character — unreliable narrator,
hidden state, no hints. You produce a workflow map, a scoping doc, and a priced proposal.
Critiqued against `rubrics/`. ~6 calls.

## Exit criteria
"I understand these businesses well enough to have an intelligent conversation with an
owner" — and to put a defensible price on the work.
